CrA results across datasets

Label-free model selection for domain generalization. CrA ranks DG models by their agreement with ERM on the unlabeled test environment. The ERM-referenced ACL estimate decides the direction: a negative estimated slope means pick the model least like ERM, a positive one means pick the model most like ERM.

True ACL: probit line of test accuracy against ID accuracy over all models (uses test labels). Estimated ACL: the same line with each model's test accuracy extrapolated along the disagreement-with-ERM axis c (no test labels). OOD estimate error is the mean absolute error of those per-model estimates.

Checks

Test labels are saved once per results folder. Every run's recorded test accuracy is recomputed from its saved predictions and these labels; if all runs reproduce it, all algorithms were scored on the same test images, which image-by-image agreement requires.

  • ColoredMNIST per-trial (env2): ✓ 360/360 runs with saved predictions reproduce their recorded test accuracy (saved per-trial label files)
  • RotatedMNIST (env5): ✗ 360/360 runs with saved predictions; recorded accuracy NOT reproduced for ERM: 60 runs, IRM: 60 runs, GroupDRO: 60 runs, CORAL: 60 runs, DANN: 60 runs, VREx: 60 runs (labels rebuilt from the dataset (none were saved))
  • ACSIncome (env9 MI): ✓ 360/360 runs with saved predictions reproduce their recorded test accuracy (saved label file)
  • PACS ResNet50 (env0): ✗ 360/360 runs with saved predictions; recorded accuracy NOT reproduced for ERM: 2 runs, IRM: 2 runs, VREx: 1 runs, CORAL: 1 runs (labels rebuilt from the dataset (none were saved))
  • PACS ResNet50 (env1): ✗ 120/120 runs with saved predictions; recorded accuracy NOT reproduced for ERM: 4 runs, IRM: 2 runs (labels rebuilt from the dataset (none were saved))
  • PACS CLIP (env0): ✓ 120/120 runs with saved predictions reproduce their recorded test accuracy (labels rebuilt from the dataset (none were saved))
  • PACS CLIP (env1): ✓ 120/120 runs with saved predictions reproduce their recorded test accuracy (labels rebuilt from the dataset (none were saved))

Methods

ATC
Average Thresholded Confidence (Garg et al. 2022), pick max
DOC
Difference of confidence, validation minus test, pick min
Nuc
Nuclear norm of the test softmax matrix, pick max
MDE
Mean dispersion energy, pick min
Dsp
Dispersion of pseudo-class centroids, pick max
Agr
Agreement with seeds of the same algorithm, pick max
TV
Training-domain validation accuracy (standard selection), pick max
MMD
MMD of test softmax to pooled ERM, direction from the estimated ACL
Wass
Wasserstein distance to pooled ERM, direction from the estimated ACL
PAD
Proxy A-distance to pooled ERM, direction from the estimated ACL
CrA
Agreement with the ERM seeds on the test images, min if the estimated ACL is negative, max if positive (ERM seeds: leave-one-out agreement with the other ERM seeds)
CrA-TV
Training-domain validation if the estimated ACL slope is positive, minimum CrA if negative
Oracle
Selects by the test environment's out-split accuracy, reports its in-split accuracy (DomainBed)

Caveats

  • ColoredMNIST is the only well-specified benchmark; it uses per-trial splits with fixed, seeded data.
  • RotatedMNIST, PACS and ACSIncome come from older single-split runs, before full determinism; to be re-run.
  • PACS ResNet50 env1 and both PACS CLIP configurations contain only ERM and IRM.
  • Label-free scores are computed on the 20% out split of the test environment; test accuracy is reported on the 80% in split.
  • The ERM row uses leave-one-out CrA.
